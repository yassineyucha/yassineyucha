About

I’m Yassine Alloul, also known as YUCHA — a 22-year-old visual creator studying Graphic Design and Advertising at Ibn Tofail University in Morocco.

My work brings together video editing, photography, photo retouching, and graphic design. I’m interested in the details that make an image feel different, the rhythm that makes a video engaging, and the visual decisions that turn an idea into something memorable.

I approach every project as an opportunity to experiment, tell a story, and create something with its own identity.

Whether it’s a cinematic edit, a promotional video, a photograph, or a complete visual concept, my goal is simple: make it meaningful, make it memorable, make it stand out.
